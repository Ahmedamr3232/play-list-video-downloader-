Step-by-Step Guide

Instructions: Download & How to Use

Easy for anyone to set up in less than 60 seconds. Toggle your preferred language below:

01

Download & Extract the Tool

Download the ZIP file from GitHub and extract it anywhere on your PC (e.g. your Downloads or Desktop folder).

Download Latest ZIP (.ZIP)
02

Run setup.bat (1-Click Installer)

Double-click setup.bat. It automatically checks Python, installs yt-dlp and dependencies, configures FFmpeg, and creates a Start YT-DLP Server shortcut on your Desktop!

Double-click: setup.bat ← Everything automated
03

Load Extension into Chrome / Edge

1. Open Chrome and go to: chrome://extensions
2. Turn ON "Developer mode" (top-right toggle)
3. Click "Load unpacked" and select the extension folder inside this repository.

chrome://extensions → Load unpacked
04

Start Downloading Anytime!

1. Double-click the "Start YT-DLP Server" icon on your Desktop.
2. Open YouTube, click the extension icon, paste your link, and click Fetch.
3. Choose Video or MP3 320k, pick your folder, and download at maximum speed!

Desktop: Start YT-DLP Server.lnk
